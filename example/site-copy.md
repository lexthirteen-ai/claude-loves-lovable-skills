# Site copy: Lather & Lane

**The one action:** join the early-access list
**Button text (everywhere):** "Get early access"

## 0. Page setup (send first)
```text
Build a single-page marketing site for Lather & Lane, online booking and driving-order day plans for solo mobile dog groomers. Follow the brand system in project knowledge for colors, fonts, spacing, and voice. Sticky top bar with "Lather & Lane" on the left and one button, "Get early access", on the right that scrolls to the section with id "cta". I will add the sections one at a time.
```

## 1. Hero
**Purpose:** say what it is, who it is for, and what to do, in five seconds.
- Headline: Book the van. Keep the calm.
- Subhead: Clients book from your link. Your day shows up in driving order. Built for solo mobile groomers.
- Button: "Get early access"
- Under the button: Free during early access. No app for your clients to download.

**Layout:** Left-aligned text on Fresh Towel, very large Bricolage Grotesque headline. On the right, a phone frame showing the Today screen. On phones, the frame drops below the button.

**Lovable prompt:**
```text
Add a hero section. Headline: "Book the van. Keep the calm." Subhead: "Clients book from your link. Your day shows up in driving order. Built for solo mobile groomers." Primary button: "Get early access" (scrolls to #cta). Small line under the button: "Free during early access. No app for your clients to download." Text on the left, an empty rounded phone frame on the right. Stack on phones.
```

  - Bookings in whatever order clients reply.
  - Two hours a day zig-zagging across town.

**Layout:** Night Route band with Fresh Towel text. Three short lines, each with a Brass Tag number (30 min, 1 text thread per client, 2 hours).

**Lovable prompt:**
```text
Add a problem section below the hero on the Night Route background with Fresh Towel text. Heading: "Your evenings shouldn't be a group text". Three rows, each a large Brass Tag number and one line: "30 min / of texting to fit tomorrow together", "1 thread / per client, in whatever order they reply", "2 hours / a day zig-zagging across town".
```

## 3. How it works
**Purpose:** make it feel easy and real.
- Heading: Three taps from booking to a planned day
- Step 1: Set your area. Pick which neighborhoods you cover on which days.
- Step 2: Share your link. Clients only see times that fit your route.
- Step 3: Open Today. Your stops, in driving order, with dog notes.

**Layout:** Three columns on desktop, stacked on phones. Each step has a large Fetch number, a bold verb phrase, and one line.

**Lovable prompt:**
```text
Add a "How it works" section. Heading: "Three taps from booking to a planned day". Three steps with large Fetch-colored numbers: (1) "Set your area" / "Pick which neighborhoods you cover on which days." (2) "Share your link" / "Clients only see times that fit your route." (3) "Open Today" / "Your stops, in driving order, with dog notes." Three columns on desktop, stacked on phones.
```
