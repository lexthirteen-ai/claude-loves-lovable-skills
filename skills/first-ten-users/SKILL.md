# First Ten Users

Building was never the wall. Distribution was, and ai made that worse by making it easy to finish things nobody ever sees. This skill makes getting users a habit that runs alongside the build, not a panic after it.

## When to use

- You built something and cannot get anyone to use it.
- You are about to build and have no plan for who will see it.
- You keep finishing projects that nobody knows exist.

## Process

1. **Name the ten.** Not a market, ten specific people or a specific place they gather. Who has the pain this solves badly enough to try something new. Where do they already talk: which communities, subreddits, discords, group chats, events.
2. **Show up with value first, for real.** Before mentioning your product, spend time being useful where those people are: answer questions, share what you have learned, publish the thing you wish existed. The ratio is heavily value first, with your product trailing softly and rarely.
3. **Build in public alongside the work.** Post what you are building and what you are learning as you go, so by the time you ship, some of the ten have been watching.
4. **Make a small, direct ask.** When you have earned it, invite specific people to try it, one at a time, personally. Ten real conversations beats one broadcast. Not a launch, an invitation.
5. **Talk to everyone who says yes.** The first users are worth more as teachers than as numbers. Ask what almost stopped them, what they expected, what would make them tell a friend. Feed that back into the product.
6. **Make it weekly.** Pick the two or three distribution actions you will repeat every week, and protect that time the way you protect building time.

## Output

- A named list of the first ten targets and where they gather.
- A weekly distribution habit: two or three repeatable actions.
- A short, honest value-first plan for each place, with the product trailing.
- A simple way to capture what early users tell you.

## Guardrails

- **Value first, always.** Communities can smell a founder who only showed up to promote. Give far more than you ask, and never post product where the rules forbid it.
- **Specific over broad.** Ten named people beats a vague audience. Resist the urge to skip to "everyone."
- **Distribution runs with the build, not after.** The habit only works if it starts before launch day.
- **No manipulation.** No fake scarcity, no bought engagement, no astroturfing. The point is real trust, which is also the moat.
